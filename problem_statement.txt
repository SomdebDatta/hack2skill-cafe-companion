The Cafe Companion

Design a smarter cafe experience powered by AI.
Cafes are places to work, meet, connect and unwind.
Your challenge is to create an application or AI agent that makes the cafe experience more personal, convenient and connected for both customers and cafe teams.

MANDATORY
Your solution must use Google skills - https://github.com/google/skills
Install the skills

Your solution could explore:
1. Smarter ORdering:
    Conversation ordering, preferences and recommendations
2. Better Waits:
    Predict wait times, order readiness or the best time to visit
3. Discovery:
    Personalised food and drink recommendations
4. Understand the Room:
    Use live feedback or sentiment to help cafe teams respond better
